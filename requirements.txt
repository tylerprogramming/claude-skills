# `python3 some_skill.py` works no matter what python3 resolves to on the PATH.
#
# Rebuild it with:  ./setup.sh
#
# Pinned exactly, not floored. This environment is synced in spirit across two
# Macs, and "works on the laptop, not the desktop" is a debugging session nobody
# wants. Bump deliberately, not by accident on a fresh install.
